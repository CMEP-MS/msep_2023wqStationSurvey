Map of 2023 WQ Station Survey Results

Background

In July of 2023, the then-developing Mississippi Sound Estuary Program sent out a survey to determine where its stakeholders wanted new monitoring stations to be located. This survey used ArcGIS Survey123 and allowed respondents to place a point on the map representing the location of a desired station. Four separate surveys allowed the identification of top priority and lower priority locations. Additional fields allowed respondents to identify which parameters they would like to see monitored at the location and why.

Map

Points on the map below were identified as locations where a monitoring station would be beneficial. Top priority stations are dark green (Priority: 1), with lighter green to yellow points representing lower priorities.
